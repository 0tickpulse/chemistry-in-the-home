As you might see from the discussion in Section 3.3, protein structure is complicated. There are an incredible amount of forces at play (try to recall all of them). In an effort to simplify how we analyze the structure of a protein, we describe it in different “levels”. At the most basic level, we simply have a linear chain of amino acids, connected by covalent peptide bonds. This is known as the primary structure.
