Example 4.1.7. Leaves and Chlorophyll.

Leaves are green because there is a pigment called chlorophyll in it. What is the approximate wavelength that chlorophyll absorbs? Reference Figure 4.1.2 and Figure 4.1.6.
🔗
Solution.
Looking at Figure 4.1.6, chlorophyll absorbs the complementary color to green — violet light, which has a wavelength of about 400 nm. Interestingly, this is not entirely accurate; chlorophyll actually absorbs both red and blue light, which mix to violet.
