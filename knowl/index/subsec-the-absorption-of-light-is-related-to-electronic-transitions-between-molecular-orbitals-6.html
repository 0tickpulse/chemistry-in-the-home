Each MO has a specific energy level, and electrons occupy them starting with the lowest energy level. They will occupy the MOs until there are no electrons left; the last electron occupies the highest occupied molecular orbital (HOMO). The orbital right above it is, as one would expect, the lowest unoccupied molecular orbital (LUMO). You can see an example of a diagram with the HOMO and LUMO labeled in Figure 4.1.4.
