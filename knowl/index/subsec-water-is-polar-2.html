To understand why tap water simply bounces off your oily hand, we have to zoom in on what water actually is. If you have seen the symbols \(\ce{H2O}\text{,}\) you know that a single water molecule is made of two hydrogen ( \(\ce{H}\) ) atoms and one oxygen ( \(\ce{O}\) ) atom. The dynamics between three atoms lead to a property called polarity, in which one side of the molecule is positively charged, and the other is negatively charged.
