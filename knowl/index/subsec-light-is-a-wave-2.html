For most of our history, we had no idea what light was. This was quite a troubling mystery — we knew nothing about light, yet our eyes used it all the time to examine the world. After centuries of work by physicists, chemists, and biologists, we now know that light is a wave (more specifically a traveling wave), and we have an understanding of how chemicals and our eyes interact with it. Let’s unpack what that means.
