    Preface Preface

    We tend to miss out on the incredible, living chemistry unfolding right under our noses. When seen from the right angle, your home is a perfect demonstration lab of a myriad of chemistry concepts. Every time you wash your hands after eating buttered popcorn, fry an egg, iron a shirt, brush your teeth, or bleach a stain, you are executing complex chemical reactions and manipulating physical forces.
    🔗
    I have specifically written this text for someone with zero background in science. No prior knowledge in chemistry is assumed, and I assure you that there will not be any dense pages of derivations or reaction schemes. Instead, I ask of you a curiosity about the various processes happening around you — why water alone cannot wash off grease; why conditioner makes hair feel silky; why steak browns while boiling meat turns gray.
    🔗
    But this does not mean we are dumbing down anything or avoiding complex topics. You will encounter molecular orbital theory, spectroscopy, protein analysis, and other topics typically learned by chemistry majors. We will try to gently introduce these topics using a intuitive physical model, and slowly level up our understanding as needed.
